# Support Policy
- For extension support, provide: extension name, Zoho CRM module, issue description, error message (if any), and your email.
- We respond to support requests within 1-2 business days.
- For urgent issues, contact our support team directly.
- We do not provide advanced troubleshooting via the chatbot. Complex issues are escalated to our engineering team.
- All support requests are logged and tracked for follow-up.

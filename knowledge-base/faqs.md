# FAQs
**Q: How much do your services cost?**
A: Pricing depends on the scope, complexity, number of modules, automation logic, and integration requirements. Contact us for a custom quote.

**Q: Do you offer free demos?**
A: Yes. We can provide a demo or consultation to walk you through our services and extensions.

**Q: Can you customize existing Zoho CRM setups?**
A: Yes. We provide Zoho CRM customization including custom modules, workflows, Deluge scripting, widgets, and API integrations.

**Q: How long does a typical project take?**
A: Timelines depend on project scope. Simple customizations take days, while complex integrations or portals may take weeks.

**Q: Do you provide post-launch support?**
A: Yes. We offer ongoing support, maintenance, and troubleshooting for all projects and extensions.

**Q: Which file storage platforms do your extensions support?**
A: We support Zoho WorkDrive, SharePoint, Google Drive, OneDrive, and Dropbox.

**Q: Can you build custom Zoho Marketplace apps?**
A: Yes. We develop, test, and publish Zoho Marketplace extensions on behalf of clients.
